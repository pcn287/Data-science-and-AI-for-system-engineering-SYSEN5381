# C-Lock GreenFeeder Emissions Data Analysis

## Overview
This report summarizes the emissions data collected from the C-Lock GreenFeeder system for FeederID 453 over a specified period from August 1, 2025, to November 1, 2025. The data includes various emissions parameters such as CO2, CH4, O2, H2, H2S, and airflow metrics.

## Key Findings

### Emission Metrics
- **Total CO2 Emissions**: The total CO2 emissions recorded during the analysis period were significant, with the highest daily emissions observed on certain days.
- **Total CH4 Emissions**: Methane emissions (CH4) also showed notable values, contributing to the overall greenhouse gas emissions.
- **O2 and Other Gases**: Oxygen (O2) levels remained consistent, with negligible emissions of H2 and H2S throughout the data collection.

### Emission Trends
- **Daily Emission Patterns**: Emissions varied significantly by time of day, with peaks typically occurring during feeding times.
- **Animal Activity**: Different animals exhibited varying emission profiles, indicating that animal behavior and diet may influence emissions.

### Interruptions and Data Quality
- **Data Interruptions**: Some data entries were marked as interrupted, which may affect the accuracy of total emissions calculations.

## Recommendations
- **Further Analysis**: Conduct a deeper analysis to correlate animal diet and emissions to identify potential strategies for emissions reduction.
- **Monitoring**: Continuous monitoring of emissions can help in understanding the long-term trends and impacts of different feeding strategies.

## Conclusion
The C-Lock GreenFeeder emissions data provides valuable insights into the emissions generated by livestock feeding. The findings highlight the need for ongoing monitoring and analysis to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions effectively.
