### Top 3 Venues:

1. **The Glasshouse Conservatory**: Elegant atmosphere, Grand vibe, and high capacity ($14,000 rental), making it the most ideal choice for their wedding.
2. **Cloverfield Farms**: Romantic setting and casual vibe ($6,000 rental) make it a great option, but considering its minimalistic nature might not suit their style.
3. **The Foundry at Millworks**: Very flexible on catering and offers an industrial-chic aesthetic ($5,000 rental), though pricing was uncertain.

### Excluded Venues:

1. **Harborside Event Center**: High outdoor ceremony terrace cost might be too expensive for their budget of $15,000.
2. **Maple Ridge Country Club**: Luxury feel and high catering costs ($28,000 rental) are not in line with their budget.
3. **Sunrise Farm & Vineyard**: Very affordable weekend rentals are attractive but may be too low-key for their desired vibe.
4. **The Ivy House**: Street parking only (no preferred vendors), which is a disadvantage.
